Design Rationale – Bole Low Group Website
1. Business Problem

Many local service business websites fail because of:

poor navigation
unclear messaging
weak calls-to-action
lack of trust-building
outdated design

This causes visitors to leave without contacting the business.

The redesign aims to solve these problems through a modern conversion-focused UI.

2. Target Audience

The primary users include:

People needing legal representation
Injury victims
Users seeking urgent legal advice
Ethiopian users looking for trustworthy law firms

Most users may not understand legal processes clearly, so the design prioritizes simplicity, trust, and easy communication.

3. UX Strategy

The website was structured to guide users toward contacting the firm as quickly as possible.

Main UX decisions include:

Above-the-fold lead form

Users can immediately submit their case without scrolling.

Repeated CTA buttons

Call-to-actions appear throughout the website to increase conversions.

Attorney imagery

Real attorney images help establish credibility and emotional trust.

Social proof

Testimonials and results build confidence for first-time visitors.

Simple navigation

Clear page structure reduces confusion and improves usability.

4. Visual Design Decisions
Colors

Dark blue and yellow were used to communicate:

trust
professionalism
authority
confidence
Typography

Large bold headings improve readability and content hierarchy.

Layout

Spacing and section grouping help users scan information quickly.

5. Conversion Optimization

The design improves conversions through:

visible contact forms
trust-building sections
simplified content flow
strong CTA placement
easy contact access
reduced cognitive overload

6. Responsiveness Consideration

Although the current submission focuses on desktop screens, the layout structure was designed with future responsive adaptation in mind.

7. Conclusion

The final design provides a modern, professional, and conversion-focused experience tailored for local Ethiopian legal service users.
